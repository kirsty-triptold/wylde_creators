TRIPTOLD x WYLDE MARKET — offline preview
=========================================

Double-click  index.html  to open the site in your browser.

Everything is self-contained: fonts, images and styles are all inside this
folder, so it works with no internet connection and nothing installed.
You can zip this folder and email it — it will open the same way for anyone.
